2Visitor
Submit an inquiry
The contact and consultation forms capture the visitor's name, email, phone, practice area and message.
submitInquiry() → submit_inquiry()
3System
Validated and filed server-side
The RPC checks name, email and message before writing. Bad input returns a clear 400 — never a silent failure.
ERRCODE 22023 · files under the signed-in user
4System
Queued with a reference number
The inquiry enters the firm's intake queue as New with a reference number, and the write is recorded in the audit trail.
inquiries · WI-YYYY-NNN · audit_logs
5Admin
Admin reviews the intake queue
Staff work the queue in the Client Intake screen; status moves through Under Review and Contacted as follow-up happens.
Admin Intake · inquiry status workflow
6Admin
Qualified → converted to a matter
One action creates the numbered matter, links the client's account and marks the inquiry Converted. No client account? The client registers with that email first.
convert_inquiry_to_matter() · INQUIRY_CONVERTED
7AdminLawyer
Lawyer and team assigned
The lead lawyer and the supporting team are attached to the matter; everyone is notified and the work begins.
matter_members · notifications
8ClientLawyer
Portal collaboration → close
Clients track the matter, message the team and exchange documents. When the work ends the matter is Resolved / Closed — audit trail complete.
matter_notes · documents · realtime
